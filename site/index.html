About & accuracy

Depths fuse ICESat-2 lidar, cleaned recreational sonar (~1M soundings), CGS survey isobaths and Sentinel-2 satellite bathymetry into one reconciled model.

Validated against held-out lidar: 0–15 m RMSE 1.47 m. The 15–25 m band is data-limited (RMSE ~4.8 m) and shown hatched as reduced-confidence until multibeam survey data is available.

Depths are negative metres below chart datum.

Not for navigation.
